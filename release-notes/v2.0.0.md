## What's new in LocalDoc Finder 2.0.0

### New: chat with your files
- **Ask questions about your documents** in plain language and get an answer written by an AI model that runs **entirely on your phone**. Every answer lists the files (and PDF pages) it was based on — tap one to open it. Open the chat from the new chat button in the top bar or from the menu → *Chat with your files*.
- **Works out of the box.** Right after installing, the default model (**Qwen2.5 0.5B Instruct**, ~550 MB) downloads in the background over Wi-Fi. You can allow mobile data or cancel from the chat screen or the notification.
- **Choose your model** (chip at the top of the chat). All are open models with open licences:

  | Model | Size | Licence | Good for |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Qwen2.5 0.5B Instruct (default) | ~550 MB | Apache-2.0 | fast answers on most phones |
  | Qwen2.5 1.5B Instruct | ~1.6 GB | Apache-2.0 | better answers, 6 GB+ RAM |
  | DeepSeek-R1 Distill Qwen 1.5B | ~1.8 GB | MIT | step-by-step reasoning (shows its thinking) |
  | TinyLlama 1.1B Chat | ~1.1 GB | Apache-2.0 | Llama-style chat |
  | SmolLM 135M Instruct | ~160 MB | Apache-2.0 | older phones |

- **Ask about one file.** In a document's details, tap the chat button to ask questions about that file only ("summarize this").
- Downloads resume after a lost connection and continue when the app is closed; models can be deleted to free space.

### Privacy
Your files, questions and answers never leave the phone. The only new network traffic is the one-time model download from Hugging Face, which goes through the same privacy gate as before (HTTPS GET from huggingface.co / hf.co only) and shows up in the Privacy report.

### Development notes
- New `engine/llm` package: `ChatModel`, `ChatPromptBuilder`, `LlmModelStore`, `LlmDownloadWorker`, `LocalLlmEngine` (MediaPipe LLM Inference `com.google.mediapipe:tasks-genai`), `DocumentChatController`; UI in `ui/components/DocumentChatScreen.kt`.
- Retrieval-augmented generation over the existing hybrid search; the prompt is fitted to the model's context window.
- Major version bump to 2.0.0 (versionCode 20000).
